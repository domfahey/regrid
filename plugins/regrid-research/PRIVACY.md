# Privacy

The Regrid Research plugin collects, stores, and sends no data of its own.

- **Lookups.** Property lookups go to Regrid's MCP server
  (https://support.regrid.com/mcp) and are handled under
  [Regrid's privacy policy](https://regrid.com/terms/privacy).
- **Maps.** Generated map pages load the Leaflet library from unpkg.com and satellite
  imagery from Esri, so those services can see the map area being viewed.
- **Credentials.** Your Regrid token stays in your host's configuration; the plugin
  never writes it to files, chat, or reports.
