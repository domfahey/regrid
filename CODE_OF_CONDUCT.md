# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code rather than
people. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable in project discussions or events.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation for
violations. Responses should be proportionate and protect affected participants.
Report abuse privately through GitHub's report-abuse controls; do not post private
evidence in public issues. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other spaces maintained by this project.


## Handling reports

Maintainers should review reports privately, limit access to people handling the
incident, and avoid publishing reporter identities or evidence without consent,
except where disclosure is legally required. Responses may include a warning,
content removal, or temporary or permanent participation restrictions depending
on severity and repeated behavior. A maintainer involved in a report should not
judge it; another uninvolved maintainer should handle it when available.

GitHub's report-abuse controls reach GitHub, not a private project-maintainer
inbox. A dedicated maintainer reporting contact has not yet been published. Do
not post sensitive reports in public issues while that contact is unavailable.
